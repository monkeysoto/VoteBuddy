VoteBuddy Autofill - one-time browser setup
===========================================

Works in Opera / Opera GX, Chrome, Edge and Brave. (Not Firefox.)

1. Open your browser's extensions page by typing this in the address bar:
      Opera / Opera GX:  opera://extensions
      Chrome:            chrome://extensions
      Edge:              edge://extensions
      Brave:             brave://extensions
2. Turn on "Developer mode" (a switch, usually top-right; on Edge it's
   on the left).
3. Click "Load unpacked".
4. Pick THIS folder (the "extension" folder that came with VoteBuddy).
5. "VoteBuddy Autofill" now shows in the list. Done.

Keep this folder somewhere permanent (not a temp folder) - the browser
loads the extension from here. If you move it, do steps 3-4 again.

Chrome/Edge may show a "Disable developer mode extensions" pop-up when
you start the browser. Just close it (don't click Disable).

AFTER AN UPDATE: on the extensions page switch VoteBuddy Autofill OFF and
back ON (or restart the browser). The reload button alone can leave the old
background part running, and then site timers won't reach the app.

What it does: when VoteBuddy opens a vote link, it fills your Minecraft
username into the username box, clicks Vote if the page has no captcha,
and reads the site's "vote again in ..." timer for the app. It does
nothing on pages VoteBuddy didn't open, and it never touches captchas.
