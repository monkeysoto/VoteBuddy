# VoteBuddy Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 27, 2026_

VoteBuddy is a small Windows app that keeps track of your Minecraft server vote links. It has no accounts, no ads tracking and no analytics. The developer does not collect, receive or store any of your data.

## What stays on your PC

These are saved only in a file on your computer (`%APPDATA%\VoteBuddy\votes.json`) and are never sent to the developer:

- the vote links you add and their timers
- your Minecraft username
- your settings, such as dark mode and the phone alert topic name

## What leaves your PC, and only when you use the feature

- **Vote pages.** When you open a vote, VoteBuddy opens that website in your browser. What happens on that website is covered by that website's own privacy policy.
- **Browser extension (optional).** The VoteBuddy Autofill extension types your username into the vote page VoteBuddy opened. Site timers it reads go only to the VoteBuddy app on your own PC, at `127.0.0.1`.
- **Phone alerts (optional, off by default).** If you turn them on, VoteBuddy sends the names and links of sites that are ready to [ntfy.sh](https://ntfy.sh), which delivers the notification to your phone. Your username is not sent. See [ntfy's privacy policy](https://ntfy.sh/docs/privacy/).
- **Update check (download version only).** The version downloaded from GitHub asks GitHub's public API whether a newer version exists. The Microsoft Store version gets updates from the Store instead.
- **Server banner.** Clicking the banner only copies a server address to your clipboard. Nothing is sent.

## Children

VoteBuddy does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
